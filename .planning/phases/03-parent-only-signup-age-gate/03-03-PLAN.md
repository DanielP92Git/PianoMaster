<objective>
Close the phase's highest-risk structural gap (RESEARCH Pitfall 1): `apiAuth.getCurrentUser()` — the single source of truth for "does this session have a profile" — currently probes only `teachers` and `students` and has zero awareness of the `parents` table. Without a third `parents` probe, every self-registered parent (email OR OAuth) resolves to `needsRoleSelection: true` on every load, looping forever. This plan adds the `parents` branch, returns `isParent`, exposes it through `useUser()`, and lands the Wave 0 unit test that does not exist today.

Purpose: SIGNUP-03's "no unguarded path to account creation" and the whole parent identity model depend on a parent account actually _resolving_ after signup. This is the contract Plan 06 (routing) and Plan 08 (OAuth completion) consume.
Output: `getCurrentUser()` detects parents; `useUser()` exposes `isParent`; direct unit coverage of the 3-table branch.
</objective>
