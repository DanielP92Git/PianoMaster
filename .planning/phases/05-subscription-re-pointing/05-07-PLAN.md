## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-5-26 | Tampering | production `parent_subscriptions` | mitigate | Enforced pre-handover check: exactly one `BEGIN;`, exactly one `ROLLBACK;`, zero `COMMIT;`. Even a mid-script `ERROR` aborts the transaction, so both the success and failure paths discard all changes |
| T-5-28 | Denial of Service | production database | accept | The rehearsal takes exclusive DDL locks on `parent_subscriptions` for the transaction's duration. | T-5-12 | Information Disclosure | `05-apply-log.md` | mitigate | The log records assertion ids, NOTICE text, and UUIDs only. The acceptance criteria assert no email addresses appear. The D-08 audit records parent UUIDs, not identities |
